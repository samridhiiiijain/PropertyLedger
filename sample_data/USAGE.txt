PropertyLedger synthetic demo dataset
===================================

All names, identifiers, dates, amounts, payment references, tax identifiers,
and document narratives in this folder were invented for demonstration.
These files are not anonymized copies of company records. Amounts are INR.

Contents
--------
structured/: 7 CSV tables containing 3 properties/projects, 5 vendors,
4 cost codes, 8 purchase orders, 8 invoices, 2 payments and 6 budgets.
documents/: 10 fictional plain-text documents with parser-compatible metadata.
generate_sample.py: deterministic generator that reads no company files.
Run `python sample_data/generate_sample.py` to regenerate this folder's data.
The generator overwrites only its own generated CSV and document filenames.

Using the dataset
-----------------
The application currently loads data/structured and data/documents; it does
not automatically load sample_data/. No application files were changed.

In a SEPARATE public checkout that contains no company data, copy the two
subfolders into data/structured and data/documents. For example, from the
root of that clean checkout in PowerShell:

New-Item -ItemType Directory -Path data -Force
Copy-Item -Recurse -LiteralPath sample_data/structured -Destination data/structured
Copy-Item -Recurse -LiteralPath sample_data/documents -Destination data/documents

Use these commands only when those destination subfolders do not yet exist.
Do not copy over or combine with the current workspace's confidential data/.
Commit sample_data/ to GitHub and keep the original data/ excluded.
Start with a fresh local index/database; rebuild document and graph indexes
when switching datasets, so prior company records cannot remain in use.

These files follow the existing CSV columns and document metadata format.
They use new identifiers, so existing tests and benchmarks that expect the
original records or exact original answers are not expected to pass unchanged.

Demo questions and expected facts
--------------------------------
Which PR901 invoices are unpaid?
  INV9101, INV9103, INV9104 and INV9108 under the application's unpaid rule.
  INV9102 is partially paid and is excluded from that classification.

Which PR901 invoices exceed their purchase orders?
  INV9101: 12,000; INV9102: 6,000; INV9104: 5,000; INV9108: 3,000.

Which PR901 cost codes are over budget?
  CC901: 15,000; CC904: 3,000.

Which documents are missing before INV9103 can proceed?
  Signed advance request and current vendor registration documentation.

Why is INV9104 on hold?
  The 45,000 invoice exceeds the 40,000 PO; a corrected invoice or written
  variance approval is required before payment.

Negative evidence examples
-------------------------
INV9108 deliberately has no invoice-specific document.
DOC-DEMO-INV9107 belongs to PR903 and must not support PR901 answers.
No bank account numbers or real personal contact details are included.

Budget conventions
------------------
For this demo, committed_amount equals the sum of recorded invoice amounts
in the project/cost-code group, including paid invoices. actual_amount equals
completed payments, and remaining_budget = budget_amount - committed_amount.
PO totals independently equal po_quantity * po_unit_rate.
